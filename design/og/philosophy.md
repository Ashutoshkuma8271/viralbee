# Honeycomb Signal: design philosophy for the Viral Bee share image

A share preview lives for half a second in a crowded feed. It must read as a single shape before it reads as words.

Space is warm darkness, not void: a honey-black field that makes the gold feel lit from within. The hexagon, the bee's own unit of architecture, appears once as structure, a faint lattice receding from the right edge, and once as punctuation in the gold band, so the brand's geometry is felt rather than announced.

Type carries the voice. A single sentence in heavy Clash Display, set large and tight, owns the left two-thirds of the canvas. Nothing competes with it: no badges, no gradients, no glow. The only colour event is the gold band along the base, the same ticker visitors meet under the website hero, so the preview and the page feel like one object.

Every element is placed with the patience of something made by hand: margins equal on every side, baseline of the headline locked to the band's top edge rhythm, the mark small and exact in the corner.
